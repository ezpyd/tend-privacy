Privacy Policy — Tend for iPhone

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Tend ("the app") is a calm weekly planner developed by Ezra Halevi ("we", "us"). This policy explains exactly what Tend for iPhone does and doesn't do with your information. It is written to be read, not to hide behind legalese.

The short version

Tend keeps your plans on your iPhone. It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no account. The only time any information leaves your phone is when you choose to send feedback. We never sell or share your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you create in Tend — your tasks, repeating tasks, weekly reflections and all settings — is stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared by the app and its widget. We have no server and no copy of it. If you delete Tend, this data is deleted from the iPhone.

One note: your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) may include a copy of your tasks. That copy belongs to your Apple account, never to us, and you control it in your iPhone's backup settings.

Tend also keeps two small facts in the iPhone's secure Keychain: the day your free trial started, and whether you have unlocked the app. They stay on this iPhone only — they are not synced to iCloud and are not carried to another phone by a backup — and, so that a trial isn't restarted by reinstalling, they remain after you delete the app. They contain nothing about you beyond those two facts.

Your calendar

If you allow calendar access, Tend reads your iPhone's calendar so it can show your events next to your tasks. This happens entirely on your iPhone. Your calendar events are never sent to us or anyone else, and never stored by Tend — they are read and shown, nothing more. Tend never changes, adds or deletes calendar events. You can decline or turn off calendar access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars; Tend keeps working without it.

The widget

Tend's Today widget shows the same tasks and events on your Home Screen and lets you tick a task done. It reads the same on-device storage as the app. Nothing about the widget leaves your iPhone.

Feedback you choose to send

Tend has an optional "Send feedback" page. It does nothing unless you write a message and tap Send. When you do, the app posts the report to a small relay we run on Cloudflare (which passes it straight on and keeps no copy), and the relay delivers it to us through a third-party messaging service, so we can read and act on it. The report contains:

  • the message you wrote;
  • your email address — only if you choose to enter one (so we can reply);
  • basic technical context to help us understand the report: the app version, your iOS version, your iPhone model, whether you are on the free trial or have unlocked the app, and the time you wrote the message.

That's the complete list. We do not attach your tasks, your calendar, your usage history, or any identifier beyond the above. If you're offline when you tap Send, the report waits on your iPhone (at most ten of them, excluded from backups) and is sent the next time you open Tend with a connection. If you never use the feedback page, nothing ever leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

Tend offers a one-time unlock after a 14-day free trial. Purchases and restores are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or receive your payment details or your Apple ID. Tend keeps only the Keychain flag described above, recording that the app is unlocked. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or usage-tracking tools, and no third-party trackers.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your personal data with anyone.
  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no cloud profile.
  • Nothing runs in the background behind your back: Tend does its work when you open it, when you change a task, and when the widget refreshes.

Permissions, briefly

  • Calendar (read only): to show your events beside your tasks, on your iPhone only. Asked only when you choose to connect your calendar.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the optional daily overview or set a reminder on a task (15, 30 or 45 minutes, or an hour before its time). Both are off by default, asked for only when you turn them on, and scheduled on your iPhone. Tend never uses badges.
  • Network: used only to deliver feedback you choose to send. The App Store handles purchases.

Data retention and deletion

Your in-app data lives on your iPhone and is removed when you delete Tend (a copy may remain in your own iPhone backup, under your control; the two Keychain facts remain on the iPhone, as explained above). Feedback you sent has already been delivered to the developer. We keep it, with no fixed end date, for as long as it helps us answer you and improve Tend, and we delete it whenever you ask. To ask us to delete feedback you sent (and any reply email you gave), contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Because Tend keeps your plans on your iPhone, the only personal information we can ever hold about you is feedback you chose to send (and your email address, if you added one). Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • see what we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • get a copy of it in a common format;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or withdraw your consent.

Email us at the address below. We answer within 30 days and don't charge for it. We will never treat you differently for asking.

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK (GDPR): we use feedback only to read it and, if you gave an email address, to reply. The legal basis is your consent, given when you tap Send, and our legitimate interest in fixing and improving the app. You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete your feedback. Feedback passes through a relay on Cloudflare (no copy kept) and is delivered through a third-party messaging service; both may process it on servers outside your country and handle it under their own privacy policies. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA): the only categories we may collect are the ones listed under "Feedback you choose to send" (your message, an optional email address, and basic device and app details), and only when you send feedback. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. You have the right to know, delete and correct it, as described above.

Children

Tend is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Tend handles information, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
